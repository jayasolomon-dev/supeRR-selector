### 5.2 Negative Sign & Differential Directionality Verification

In cricket analytics, bowling differentials require careful sign interpretation:
$$\Delta\text{Economy} = \text{Economy}_{\text{high}} - \text{Economy}_{\text{normal}}$$
- In T20 cricket, high-pressure regimes (overwhelmingly death overs) typically exhibit higher scoring rates than normal-pressure regimes. The global population mean economy differential is $+1.83$ runs per over.
- Therefore:
  - A **negative** economy differential ($\Delta\text{Econ} < 0$) indicates exceptional performance: the bowler conceded fewer runs per over under pressure than in normal play.
    - Example: Haris Rauf ($\text{shrunk\_diff\_economy\_rate} = -1.12$), Wanindu Hasaranga ($-0.64$), Kartik Tyagi ($-0.43$).
  - A **mildly positive** economy differential ($\Delta\text{Econ} \in [0, +0.81]$) represents elite run containment relative to the $+1.83$ baseline surge.
    - Example: Mitchell Marsh ($+0.30$), Vaibhav Arora ($+0.28$), Azmatullah Omarzai ($+0.12$).
- In the authoritative 4-component production bowling reliability formula (`src/analytics/task_05_reliability.py`, lines 359–371):
  $$Z_{\text{bowl}} = 0.40 \cdot Z_{\Delta\text{Econ}} + 0.25 \cdot Z_{\Delta\text{Wkt}} + 0.20 \cdot Z_{\Delta\text{Dot}} + 0.15 \cdot Z_{\text{Econ\_high}}$$
  Where:
  $$Z_{\Delta\text{Econ}} = -\frac{\text{shrunk\_diff\_economy\_rate} - \mu_{\Delta\text{Econ}}}{\sigma_{\Delta\text{Econ}}}$$
  $$Z_{\text{Econ\_high}} = -\frac{\text{shrunk\_economy\_rate\_high} - \mu_{\text{Econ\_high}}}{\sigma_{\text{Econ\_high}}}$$
  The negative directionality signs ensure that:
  1. A **lower economy differential** (suppressing run rate inflation under pressure) contributes positively to $Z_{\text{bowl}}$ and player reliability.
  2. A **lower absolute high-pressure economy** (conceding fewer runs under death/high pressure) also contributes positively to $Z_{\text{bowl}}$ and player reliability.
  *(Note: An earlier 3-component formula, $\text{Composite } Z = -0.50 \cdot Z(\Delta\text{Econ}) + 0.30 \cdot Z(\Delta\text{WPO}) + 0.20 \cdot Z(\Delta\text{Dot\%})$, was an outdated theoretical specification from initial planning. It was not used in production and was not used to generate the certified rankings.)*
